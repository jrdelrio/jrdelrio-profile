# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences of equal weight:
- **Recruiters and hiring teams** (startups and companies) evaluating Raimundo as a full-stack developer.
- **Business owners and founders** who need a website, often small and medium Chilean businesses or people starting a company from the digital side.

Both must leave convinced of professionalism and competence.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Raimundo del Río, full-stack web developer and founder of Chilisites. It tells his story, shows real shipped work with the stack behind each project, and converts visitors into job conversations or client quotes (contact form, email, WhatsApp, AI chat).

## Positioning

A developer who owns the whole project end to end: original design, development, self-managed content, domain, business email, hosting and maintenance. Through Chilisites he can then add SEO and paid campaigns to bring clients. Experience building product abroad (Spain), now serving the Chilean market.

## Operating Context

- Story: 2 years in Spain as a developer at Compensa Capital Humano (HR management platform) → back in Santiago in 2025 (his hometown) → founded Chilisites (web design/development + digital marketing: Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok).
- Visitors scan the home, check projects and stack, then contact or chat.
- Blog: his own Medium articles (https://medium.com/@jrdelriodom), read automatically from the RSS feed.
- AI chat (Gemini) answers in first person as Raimundo; its prompt is built from the same project and service data as the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack offered: Next.js + Tailwind + Sanity, Astro + Tailwind, Next.js/Astro + Sanity, Next.js + Tailwind + Django; Flask, PostgreSQL, Supabase; Resend, Zoho.
- Shopify and WordPress: available when the client needs them, never presented as his strength.
- Not offered: React Native / native mobile apps, Strapi, GraphQL.
- **Never name Vercel** anywhere visitor-facing (site copy, chat). Hosting is described as managed hosting. AWS/Google Cloud only if a client asks; never offered up front.
- Bilingual ES (default) / EN via client-side dictionary.
- Projects and services are data files (`src/data/projects.ts`, `src/data/services.ts`); adding a project = one object + one screenshot.
- Public domain not decided yet (`SITE_URL`).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Raimundo del Río. Company: Chilisites.
- Voice: close but serious; professional, technical, intelligent and friendly. Never jokey.
- Visual design is open to iteration; no binding palette or typography.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real projects (all live): Décima Propiedades, Centro Benesseri, Granasur, Abaqus, Chilisites (home); Andiamoo, ALPS Payments, 332 Arquitectos, La Birra Esquina.
- Screenshots in `src/assets/projects/`. Profile photos: `src/assets/profile.png`, `public/portrait.jpg`. CV: `public/CV_Jose_Raimundo_del_Rio_Domeyko.pdf`.
- He did not run marketing on the portfolio projects; do not attribute campaign results to them. Marketing is a Chilisites service.
- No testimonials, metrics, client logos or pricing exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: real projects with real stack beat adjectives.
2. Precision over volume: few elements, each informative.
3. Speak to both audiences without splitting the site in two.
4. Every section leads toward a quote or a conversation.
5. Honesty about the stack: say what is and isn't offered.
